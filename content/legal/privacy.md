# Privacy Policy

## 1. What we collect

- Contact details you give us (name, email, phone) when you send an enquiry
  or create an account.
- Documents you upload for review, editing feedback, or as evidence of your
  own authorship.
- Basic usage data from our free tools, if you choose to email yourself a
  result.

## 2. What we don't do

We don't sell your data. We don't share your documents with anyone outside
the team member or mentor assigned to help you.

## 3. How long we keep it

Uploaded documents are kept for a limited retention period (default 90
days; see BUILD_SPEC.md Section 9.9, still to be confirmed) unless you
delete them sooner from your account, or ask us to.
